# Application field contract

Classify every field before filling it.

## Confirmed factual fields

Fill from the confirmed profile: name, contact details, education, employment history, demonstrated skills, and confirmed certifications. Preserve exact dates and wording where precision matters.

## Job-specific factual fields

Ask when not already confirmed: notice period, start date, salary expectation, willingness to relocate or travel, shift availability, and preferred location.

## Sensitive or legal fields

Require a direct user answer or confirmation for work authorization, sponsorship, disability or health information, demographics, criminal history, veteran status, conflicts of interest, background-check consent, voluntary self-identification, and legal attestations. Do not infer an answer or default to “prefer not to say.”

## Narrative fields

Draft motivation, relevant-experience, company-interest, cover-letter, and additional-information answers only from confirmed evidence. Keep claims truthful and proportional. Show the draft before entering it when it contains a new formulation or employer-specific claim.

## Submission controls

Treat controls labelled Submit, Send application, Confirm and submit, Finish, Complete application, Apply, or equivalents as final until proven otherwise. A general “prepare these jobs” instruction never authorizes a final submission.

If an earlier action can submit automatically, stop before it. When uncertain, hand control to the user.
