# Search filter questions

Ask naturally in small groups and skip values already confirmed. Each question accepts **Suggest for me**, **No preference**, or **Skip**.

1. Target titles, or permission to suggest related roles from the confirmed profile.
2. Search cities, regions, or countries.
3. Acceptable on-site, hybrid, remote, field, or event arrangements.
4. Acceptable permanent, contract, temporary, part-time, freelance, or internship work.
5. Experience levels to include.
6. Minimum salary as a hard floor and preferred salary separately.
7. Mandatory qualifications, skills, licences, or certifications.
8. Language requirements to include or exclude.
9. Industries or companies to include or exclude.
10. Company career pages, job boards, or both; recommend both when undecided.
11. Posting age; recommend prioritizing 7 days while allowing older verified-open roles.
12. Commute origin and maximum one-way time when relevant.
13. Other automatic exclusions such as shifts, travel, or commission-only pay.
14. Ranking preferences such as benefits, training, or flexible hours.
15. Number of verified results; recommend 10 when undecided.

An AI suggestion remains a preference until the user confirms it as a must-have. Never invent a market salary; when asked to suggest one, verify current evidence and label its source and date.

Before searching, show **Must have** and **Prefer** lists and ask for confirmation. If a must-have is ambiguous, resolve it first.
