# Find My Job portable prompt

Use this in an AI that cannot install the plugin:

> Help me find current jobs or internships. First onboard me conversationally: qualifications (including SPM/IGCSE and higher), field, target titles or qualification-based discovery, languages and mandatory/preferred language exclusions, relevant experience or fresh-graduate status, minimum and preferred salary, preferred sources, acceptable work arrangements, benefits, search location, commute origin, and hard requirements versus preferences. Web search is the baseline. Verify that every ranked vacancy is open, prefer posts from the last 7 days, record exact advertised salary and public contact details without guessing, link the direct application page, and deduplicate. Rank only confirmed hard-criteria matches; ask permission before relaxing a hard criterion. Show exactly six columns: # | Job title / Company | Salary | Work arrangement | Contact | Apply. Put any verified commute details below the table. Never apply without my approval. After the first search, optionally offer a weekday 08:00 Asia/Kuala_Lumpur new-results-only schedule if scheduling is supported.

This prompt preserves the core workflow but may not persist preferences, run helper scripts, or create schedules on every AI.
